Mood imperative
===============

Glossary
--------

The **imperative mood** is is a mood of direction.  It includes commands, and orders, but also requests and directions.  
It expresses the intentions of the speaker and appeals to the will of the hearer.  

Example V-IM- 1

.. csv-table:: V-IM-1 Matthew 26:26

  **λάβετε**,**φάγετε**,τοῦτό,ἐστιν,τὸ,σῶμά,μου 
  **labete**,**phagete**,touto,estin,to,sōma,mou.
  **You take**,**you eat**,this,it is,the,body,of me 

**Take.Eat.** *This is my body.*
