.. _adverb:

Adverb
======

Summary
-------

.. include:: en_uhg/content/includes/adverb-summary.rst
.. include:: includes/adverb-summary-greek.rst

Adverbs are used to express relationships of place, time, circumstance, manner, cause, or degree. Generally adverbs answer the question
  *  How?
  *  When?
  *  Where?
  *  How much?
  *  Why? , or 
  *  To what extent?

Article
-------
In Koiné Greek, adverbs can be formed from any number of different kinds of words and word endings.
However, they do not decline like nouns or adjectives. Any one particular adverb usually has only one single form.

  The most common way to form an adverb is to take an genitive plural adjective in the positive degree and replace the nu ending **"ν"** with a sigma **"ς"**.
  For example:   καλὸς (good or beautiful) -> καλῶν (genitive plural) -> καλῶς (well).

How ever there are many ways of forming an adverb:
  1)  Adverbs can be formed from the acusative case (e.g.)  
      πέραν -beyond; δωρέαν - freely: χάριν - for the sake of

  2)  Others from the genitive (e.g.)
      ὀμοῦ - together;  ὅπου - where (designating a location);  or ποῦ - where? 

  3)  Dative (e.g.)
      ἐλεῖ - there
      πρωἰ - early
      ἕξω  - without
      ἄμα  - together

  4)  Adverbs can be formed by adding suffixes that give additional meaning

    a)  -ι,-σι,-ου  relates to a **place** such as:
        ὅπου - where,   ἐκεῖ -τηερε
        
    b)  -θεν relates to a **place from**  such as:
        ἅνωθεν - from above
        
    c)  -δε,-σε  relates to a **direction** such as:
        ἐκεῖσε - to that place, over there
        
    d)   -τε relates to **time**, such as:
         ὅτε -when,    τότε - then
         
    e)  -ως  relates to a **manner**, such as:
        ταχέως - quickly
        
    f)  -ις, -κις relates to a **number**, such as:
        δίς - twice, τρίς - three times

      
Adverbs describes a verb, adjective, or other adverb
--------------------------------------------
There are several different ways that an adverb can describe a verb. 

1) Locative - Adverbs may describes a place or location
~~~~~

A ``locative adverb`` describes something about the *place* or *location* where the action of the verb occurs.


Example AD-1 Locative adverb
^^^^^

.. csv-table::  Matthew 8:29

  ἦλθες,**ὧδε**,πρὸ,καιροῦ,βασανίσαι,ἡμᾶς
  ēlthes,**hōde**,pro,kairou,basanisai,hēmas
  have you come,**here**,before,time,to torment,us

*Have you come* **here** *to torment us before the set time?*   

Example AD-2 Locative adverb
^^^^^

.. csv-table:: Matthew 2:15

  καὶ,ἦν,**ἐκεῖ**,ἕως,τῆς,τελευτῆς,Ἡρῴδου
  kai,ēn,**ekei**,heōs,tēs,teleutēs,Hērōdou
  and,he was,**there**,until,the,death,of Herod

*and he was* **there** *until the death of Herod*

Example AD-3 Locative adverb
^^^^^

.. csv-table:: Mark 15:38

  καὶ,τὸ,καταπέτασμα,τοῦ,ναοῦ,ἐσχίσθη,εἰς,δύο,ἀπ’,**ἄνωθεν**,ἕως,**κάτω**
  kai,to,katapetasma,tou,naou,eschisthē,eis,dyo,ap’,**anōthen**,heōs,**katō**
  and,the,curtain,of the,temple,was torn,in,two,from,**top**,to,**bottom**

*And the curtain of the temple was torn in two from* **top** *to* **bottom**

2) Temporal - Adverbs
~~~~~

A ``temporal adverb`` describes something about the *time* when the action of the verb occurs.

Example AD-4 Temporal adverb
^^^^^

.. csv-table::  Mark 10:30

  ἐὰν,μὴ,λάβῃ,ἑκατονταπλασίονα,**νῦν**,ἐν,τῷ,καιρῷ,τούτῳ
  ean,mē,labē,hekatontaplasiona,**nyn**,en,tō,kairō,toutō
  if,not,he will receive,a hundred times as much,**now**,in,the,time,this

*he will not receive a hundred times as much* **now** *in this time*

Example AD-5 Temporal adverb 
^^^^^^

.. csv-table:: Matthew 4:17

  Ἀπὸ,**τότε**,ἤρξατο,ὁ,Ἰησοῦς,κηρύσσειν,καὶ,λέγειν
  apo,**tote**,ērxato,ho,Iēsous,kēryssein,kai,legein
  from,**then**, he began,the, Jesus,to preach,and,to say
  
*from* **that time** *Jesus began to preach and to say*

Example AD-6 Temporal adverb
^^^^^

.. csv-table:: Temporal  Mathew 17:12

  λέγω,δὲ,ὑμῖν,ὅτι,Ἠλείας,**ἤδη**,ἦλθεν
  legō,de,hymin,hoti,Ēleias,**ēdē**,ēlthen
  I say,but,to you,that,Elijah,**already**,he has come

*But I say to you that Elijah* **already** *came*  

3) Quantative Adverbs
~~~~~

A ``quantitative adverb`` describes an *amount* of something in relation to the action of the verb.

Example AD-7 Quantative Adverb
^^^^^

.. csv-table:: Matthew 2:10

  ἰδόντες,δὲ,τὸν,ἀστέρα,ἐχάρησαν,χαρὰν,μεγάλην,**σφόδρα.**
  idontes,de,ton,astera,echarēsan,charan,megalēn,**sphodra.**
  seeing,but,the,star,they rejoiced,joy,great,**exceedingly.**

*And having seen the star, they rejoiced with* **very** *great joy.*

[NOTE:]   In this example, "μεγάλην" (great) is an adjective describing  χαρὰν (joy).  σφόδρα (exceedingly) is a quantative adverb modifying μεγάλην.  It describes and exceedingly great joy. 

Example AD-8 Quantative Adverb
^^^^^

.. csv-table:: Maττ 7:11

   πόσῳ,**μᾶλλον**,ὁ,Πατὴρ,ὑμῶν,ὁ,ἐν,τοῖς,οὐρανοῖς,δώσει,ἀγαθὰ,τοῖς,αἰτοῦσιν,αὐτόν
   posō,**mallon**,ho,Patēr,hymōn,ho,en,tois,ouranois,dōsei,agatha,tois,aitousin,auton
   How much,**more**,the,Father,your,who,in,the,heavens,will give,good things,to those,who ask,him

*how much* **more** *will your Father in the heavens give good things to the ones asking him*
   
4) Adverb of Manner
~~~~~

An ``adverb of manner`` describes something about *how* the action of the verb happens.

Example AD-9 Adverb of Manner 
^^^^^

.. csv-table::  John 20:4

  καὶ,ὁ,ἄλλος,μαθητὴς,προέδραμεν,**τάχιον**,τοῦ,Πέτρου
  kai,ho,allos,mathētēs,proedramen,**tacheion**,tou,Petrou
  and,the,other,disciple,ran ahead,**faster**,than,Peter

*and the other disciple* **quickly** *ran ahead of Peter*
   
Example AD-10  Adverb of Manner
^^^^^

.. csv-table:: Matthew 15:7

  ὑποκριταί,**καλῶς**,ἐπροφήτευσεν,περὶ,ὑμῶν,Ἠσαΐας
  hypokritai,**kalōs**,eprophēteusen,peri,hymōn,Ēsaias
  hypocrits,**rightly**, he prophesied,about,you all,Elijah

*You hypocrites!* **Well** *did Isaiah prophesy about you*

Example AD-11 Adverb of Manner
^^^^^

.. csv-table:: Luke 2:13

  καὶ,**ἐξαίφνης**,ἐγένετο,σὺν,τῷ,ἀγγέλῳ,πλῆθος,στρατιᾶς,οὐρανίου
  kai,**exaiphnēs**,egeneto,syn,tō,angelō,plēthos,stratias,ouraniou
  and,**suddenly**,there was,with,the,angel,multitude,army,of heavenly

*And* **suddenly** *a multitude of the heavenly army was with the angel*

5) Sentential Adverbs
~~~~~

A ``sentential adverb`` describes an entire clause or sentence rather than an individual verb.
Sentential adverbs are closely related to `Conjunctions <https://ugg.readthedocs.io/en/latest/conjunction.html>`_.

Example AD-12  Sentential Adverb
^^^^^

.. csv-table::  Matthew 7:5

  καὶ,**τότε**,διαβλέψεις,ἐκβαλεῖν,τὸ,κάρφος,ἐκ,τοῦ,ὀφθαλμοῦ,τοῦ,ἀδελφοῦ,σου
  kai,**tote**,diablepseis,ekbalein,to,karphos,ek,tou,ophthalmou,tou,adelphou,sou
  and,**then**,you will see clearly, to remove,the,small piece of wood,out,of,the,eye,of,brother,your

*and* **then** *you will see clearly to take out the speck of wood from the eye of your brother*

Example AD-13 Sentential adverb
^^^^^

.. csv-table:: Matthew 26:65

  ἴδε,**νῦν**,ἠκούσατε,τὴν,βλασφημίαν 
  ide,**nyn**,ēkousate,tēn,blasphēmian
  Behold,**now**,you all have heard,the,blasphame

*Behold*, **now** *you have heard the blasphemy.*
